# Braintrust

## Who

> [!claude] Claude summary
> Evaluation and observability tooling for LLM applications — logging traces,
> comparing prompts and models, measuring output quality before and after release.
> Founded August 2023 by Ankur Goyal, previously co-founder of Impira and head of
> the ML platform at Figma; San Francisco. Beyond the core product they've built
> Brainstore, a database for observability data, and a gateway proxy for routing
> model calls. Raised an $80M Series B in February 2026 at a reported $800M
> valuation, with Airtable, Zapier, Coda and Instacart named as customers.
>
> Read their writing with the business in mind: an eval vendor arguing that agent
> quality is a measurement problem is not a neutral party.
