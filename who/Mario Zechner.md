# Mario Zechner

## Who

> [!agent] Claude summary
> Austrian software engineer, online as `badlogic`, and the author of Pi. Known
> long before this for libGDX, the cross-platform Java game framework that became
> the most used game framework on Android and shipped titles including Ingress and
> Slay the Spire.
>
> Pi's origin explains its shape: he adopted Claude Code in April 2025, then grew
> frustrated as rapid feature additions brought instability and behaviour changes
> he hadn't asked for. Pi, first released August 2025, is the reply — four built-in
> tools and a TypeScript extension system for everything else. His rule, "if I
> don't need it, it won't be built", is the clearest statement of the minimal
> position in this vault and the direct inverse of LangChain's.
>
